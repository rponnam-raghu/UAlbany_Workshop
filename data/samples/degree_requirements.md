# Workshop University Computer Science Major

This document is fictional workshop material. It is not an official University at Albany requirement sheet.

## Major overview

The fictional B.S. in Computer Science major requires 45 credits. Graduation also requires 120 total university credits, including credits outside the major.

## Core requirements

Complete CSI 201 Introduction to Programming, CSI 213 Data Structures, CSI 310 Algorithms, CSI 333 Systems, CSI 400 Software Engineering, and CSI 445 Databases. Complete two 400-level CSI electives from the approved catalog.

## Mathematics

Complete MAT 112 Calculus I, MAT 214 Discrete Mathematics, and MAT 308 Linear Algebra.

## Practice and capstone

Complete CSI 190 Computing Seminar, CSI 290 Collaborative Practice, and CSI 390 Internship Preparation. CSI 499 Senior Capstone is 3 credits and requires senior standing with at least 90 total credits and completed CSI 400.

## Advising boundary

This sheet describes requirements for planning. A human academic advisor and the registrar handle exceptions, waivers, enrollment approvals, and graduation certification.
