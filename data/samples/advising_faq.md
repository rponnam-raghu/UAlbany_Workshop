# Advising FAQ

This FAQ is fictional workshop content. It is designed for practicing retrieval and citations.

## Changing a course plan

Students may discuss a course-plan change with an advisor before registering. The helpdesk can explain prerequisites and catalog offerings, but a human advisor handles exceptions and approval decisions.

## Course eligibility

Eligibility depends on the completed courses and the current catalog. A course that is offered in a particular semester may still be unavailable to a student who has not completed its prerequisites.

## Graduation questions

The major requirement and the 120-credit graduation requirement are separate checks. Students should ask an advisor to review the official academic record before treating a planning answer as a graduation decision.

## Contact

Email fictional advising questions to advising@workshop.example. Include the topic, the term you are planning for, and the relevant course code. Do not email personal transcripts to this workshop application.
