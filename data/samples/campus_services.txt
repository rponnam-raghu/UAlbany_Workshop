FICTIONAL WORKSHOP UNIVERSITY
Campus services guide

This guide is fictional workshop material and is not official University at Albany information.

STUDENT HELP DESK
The student help desk is in Student Hub, Room 101. It is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Staff can help students find campus services and book a meeting with an academic advisor. Contact helpdesk@workshop.example.

COMPUTING SUPPORT
For help with a campus account or classroom software, contact computing@workshop.example. Include a description of the problem and the application name. Never include passwords or API keys in a support request.

STUDY SUPPORT
The Learning Center offers fictional peer tutoring in introductory programming and mathematics. Students can request a tutoring appointment at tutoring@workshop.example. Include the course code and the topic they want to practice.

ACCESSIBILITY SUPPORT
Students can contact access@workshop.example to discuss accessible learning arrangements with a human staff member. The chatbot can explain this contact information but cannot approve arrangements or exceptions.
