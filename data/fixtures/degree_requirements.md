# Fictional B.S. in Computer Science

**Workshop fiction — not the UAlbany degree sheet or official academic advice.**

- **Core (24 cr):** CSI 201, CSI 213, CSI 310, CSI 333, CSI 400, CSI 445, and two distinct electives from CSI 410, CSI 420, CSI 430. Each course is 3 credits.
- **Math (12 cr):** MAT 112, MAT 214, MAT 308. Each course is 4 credits.
- **Professional practice (6 cr):** CSI 190 (1), CSI 290 (2), CSI 390 (3).
- **Capstone (3 cr):** CSI 499. Requires CSI 400 and senior standing (90 completed overall credits).
- **Major total: 45 credits. Graduation requires 120 overall credits.**

Only completed courses earn credit or satisfy prerequisites. Repeated courses count once. Additional electives beyond the two required count toward overall credits, not extra major credit. Advising calculations never approve waivers, transcript changes, or graduation.
